A luxury spa and aesthetic beauty lounge website designed with warm golden hues, elegant serif typography, and an interactive online appointment booking workflow.

## ✨ Features

- **Luxury Aesthetic**: Warm gold and rose-gold palette, elegant typography, serene mood imagery.
- **Service Menu & Pricing**: Comprehensive catalog for Hair Styling, Skin Care, Massage Therapies, and Nail Art.
- **Interactive Booking System**: Step-by-step appointment scheduling modal with date, time slot, specialist selection, and live price estimation.
- **Client Testimonials & Gallery**: Interactive review carousel and curated treatment gallery.
- **Specialist Team Cards**: Profiles for beauty experts and therapists with their specialties.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Markup**: HTML5.
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS3, Grid/Flexbox, Keyframes, Custom Shadows and Glass Effects.
- **Logic**: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), Modal Dialog State Management, Date validations.
- **Icons**: Font Awesome 6.
